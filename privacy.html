Privacy Policy

YouTube Channel Manager · last updated 20 November 2026

Who uses this tool

YouTube Channel Manager is a private tool used only by its owner to manage the owner's own YouTube channels. It is not offered to other people, and no other users sign in to it.

What data it accesses

When the owner authorizes one of their channels, the tool receives Google OAuth tokens for that channel. With them it can upload videos, set video details (title, description, tags, thumbnail, privacy and scheduled publishing time) and read basic channel and video information, such as the channel ID and the status of uploaded videos.

How data is stored

OAuth tokens and upload records are stored only on the owner's own computer, in encrypted backups protected by the operating system. Nothing is stored on any server operated by this tool.

Sharing

The tool does not sell, rent or share any Google user data with third parties, does not use it for advertising, and does not transfer it except to Google's own YouTube services to carry out the owner's requests.

Google API Services User Data Policy

YouTube Channel Manager's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The tool uses YouTube API Services and is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account's security settings (Third-party apps with account access), which revokes the tokens. The owner can also delete the locally stored tokens and records.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact options on the owner's YouTube channels.
