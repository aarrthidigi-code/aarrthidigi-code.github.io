YouTube Channel Manager

YouTube Channel Manager is a private tool that its owner uses to upload, schedule and manage videos on the owner's own YouTube channels, such as Then Came Today and Teraflop Times. It is not offered to the public, and no one else signs in to it.

The tool uses the YouTube Data API only for the channels its owner has authorized: it uploads videos, sets their titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails and publishing times, and reads basic channel and video information to confirm the right channel is being used.

See the privacy policy for how account data is handled.
